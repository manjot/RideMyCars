## 📋 Store Release Notes (What's New)
*(Copy and paste directly into Google Play Console -> Releases -> Release Notes -> `en-US` and App Store Connect -> What's New in This Version)*

```text
• Real-Time In-App Trip Chat: Message your assigned driver or chauffeur directly inside active ride, delivery, and rental bookings.
• Whole-Number Transparent Pricing: Clean, upfront rounded fares and localized multi-currency formatting across all booking categories.
• Enhanced California & Regional Compliance: Updated consumer disclosures, safety notices, and zero-tolerance policy access.
```
