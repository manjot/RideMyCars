# 🚀 RideMyCars — Release Notes (Version 1.0.2 / Build 6)

**Release Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Target Platforms:** Google Play Store & Apple App Store  
**Version:** `1.0.2`  
**Version Code / Build Number:** `6`

---

## 📱 1. RideMyCars (Rider App — `com.ridemycars.app`)

### 📋 Google Play Store Release Notes (What's New)
```text
• Integrated SOS Safety Suite: One-tap emergency alert trigger and trusted emergency contacts management.
• In-App Wallet: Top up your balance, view transaction history, and pay seamlessly for all rides and rentals.
• New Service Catalogs: Explore Dedicated Hourly Chauffeurs, Luxury Car Rentals, and Express Package Delivery.
• Live Delivery Tracker: Track package dispatches with live driver location and status updates.
• UI & Performance: Faster loading, smoother map transitions, and enhanced location accuracy.
```

### 📋 Full Release Summary
- **SOS Emergency Suite:** Added floating emergency SOS action button, custom emergency contact management, and live SMS dispatch with GPS coordinates.
- **In-App Digital Wallet:** Added real-time wallet balance, deposit methods, transaction logs, and one-tap checkout for rides, car rentals, and delivery.
- **Hourly Chauffeurs & Car Rental:** Integrated dedicated vehicle catalog screens, detailed rental specs, and digital voucher confirmation.
- **Delivery Service:** Added parcel delivery booking and dedicated real-time courier tracking screen.
- **International Country Engine:** Automated country/currency detection with calibrated multi-tier pricing.

## 🚗 2. RideMyCars Driver (Driver App — `com.ridemycars.driver`)

### 📋 Google Play Store Release Notes (What's New)
```text
• Integrated Driver SOS Suite: Quick-access safety button with emergency contact alerting.
• Driver Earnings & Wallet: Real-time earnings breakdown, withdrawal requests, and payout method management.
• Improved Dispatch Matching: Smarter proximity routing for rides, rental deliveries, and parcel drops.
• Battery & Location Optimization: Lower battery consumption during background GPS tracking.
• Bug fixes and stability improvements.
```

### 📋 Full Release Summary
- **Driver SOS Feature:** Integrated emergency SOS button on dashboard and active trip screens with automated SMS broadcasting.
- **Earnings & Payouts:** Upgraded driver earnings dashboard with daily/weekly performance analytics, instant payout requests, and bank/mobile money setup.
- **Multi-Service Dispatching:** Enhanced driver dispatches across ride-hailing, vehicle deliveries, and parcel delivery trips.
- **Stability & Performance:** Optimized turn-by-turn navigation overlay, fixed Gradle build configuration properties, and upgraded plugin dependencies.
