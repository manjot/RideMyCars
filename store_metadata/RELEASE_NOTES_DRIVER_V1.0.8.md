## 📋 Store Release Notes (What's New)
*(Copy and paste directly into Google Play Console -> Release Notes -> `en-US` and App Store Connect -> What's New in This Version)*

```text
• Native Background & Lock-Screen Order Alerts: Full-screen incoming order call alerts with continuous ringtone and vibration even when the app is closed or the device is locked.
• Real-Time In-App Rider Chat: Instant two-way messaging with passengers directly from the active trip dashboard.
• Independent Chauffeur Availability Switch: Toggle luxury chauffeur and hourly private driver availability independently on the dashboard.
• Improved Document & Guarantor Verification: Seamless upload and authentication for Vehicle Roadworthiness, Insurance, Driver License, and Guarantor forms.
• Cash Commission Wallet & Instant Withdrawals: Track cash fares, settle platform commissions, and request immediate payouts.
• Whole-Number Multi-Currency Earnings: Clean, rounded fare calculations and localized currency formatting across trips and incentives.
```
